Common questions

A link isn't working

Sites move around and get blocked a lot. Try the other links listed on that site's page first. If none of them work, tell us below and we'll swap them out.

How do I suggest a game or a site?

Use the form below and pick Suggest a link. Include the address and, for games, the genre you think it belongs in.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. An account just remembers your theme, accent colour and layout, and brings them with you on other devices.

I forgot my password

On the log in screen choose Forgot password?. If you added an email to your account and verified it (Account window, Email section), we send a code to it. If you didn't, send an Account help message below and the admin can sort it out.

How do I change how the site looks?

Open Customize (the gear in the sidebar) to switch between light and dark, pick an accent colour, and change the layout density.

Send a message

You need an account to send a message, so we know who to reply to.

                How it works

                What is Whiskered?

                Whiskered is a curated directory of creative platforms, gaming hubs, and community resources. It helps users discover accessible browser games, unblocked sites, and themed web collections in one polished interface.

                1. 1ChoosePick Games or Websites from the home screen.
                2. 2ProwlBrowse curated categories and individual platform panels.
                3. 3PounceEvery entry has a name, icon, tagline, and direct links.

                Good to know

                • Change the theme and accent colour with Customize in the top right.
                • Big genres have a search box to find a game fast.
                • Press Esc to close any pop-up.
