    What is Whiskered?

    Whiskered is a curated directory of creative platforms, gaming hubs, and community resources. It helps users discover accessible browser games, unblocked sites, and themed web collections in one polished interface.

    1. 1ChoosePick Games or Websites from the home screen.
    2. 2ProwlBrowse curated categories and individual platform panels.
    3. 3PounceEvery entry has a name, icon, tagline, and direct links.

    Good to know

    • 🐾Change the theme and accent colour with Customize in the top right.
    • 🔍Big genres have a search box to find a game fast.
    • ⇥Press Esc to close any pop-up.
